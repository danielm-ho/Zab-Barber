The Barber

Draft — confirm exact wording with Roman

Roman didn't start in a barbershop. He started on Instagram, teaching himself technique one video at a time after his wife gave him a clipper kit for his birthday — back when he was still living in Ukraine. Within a few years, he'd opened his own shop there.

When Roman came to the U.S. as a refugee, he started over completely — new country, new clients, same standard. He's been cutting professionally for seven years now, every bit of it solo.

What sets his work apart: European-trained technique, a habit of finishing with shears instead of relying on clippers alone, and an eye for detail his 15,000+ clients keep coming back for — including professional athletes and touring musicians.

"I have your new style."

— what Roman tells every client before he picks up the clippers

That same standard now shapes how he teaches. Former students from his shop in Ukraine are still cutting professionally today — and he's building out 1-on-1 mentorship for barbers here who want the same level taught right.
